Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026 · Last updated: 2 November 2026

CustomPlate ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process, and protect personal information when you interact with our website, Instagram account, automated messaging assistant, or vehicle number plate ordering services.

1. Information We Collect

Instagram information: Instagram username, Instagram-scoped user ID provided through Meta's APIs, messages and conversation history sent to our account, and information you voluntarily provide.

Order information: Customer name, mobile number, vehicle type, requested number plate text or design details, delivery address and PIN code, order status, and order history.

Payment information: Razorpay payment ID, amount, currency, payment status, and QR/order reference information. Payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not intentionally collect or store your full card number, UPI PIN, banking password, or payment authentication credentials.

Technical information: Webhook event data, message identifiers, timestamps, and security verification information needed to operate and protect the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to Instagram messages and customer enquiries.
  • Operate our automated customer support and sales assistant.
  • Understand order requirements and process custom number plate orders.
  • Generate payment QR codes and verify payments.
  • Send order details and payment notifications to our business email.
  • Maintain conversation history and relevant non-sensitive customer preferences.
  • Provide support, handle complaints, prevent fraud, and maintain service security.

3. AI-Powered Messaging

Our Instagram assistant uses artificial intelligence to help answer customer questions and manage orders. Relevant conversation history, order information, and saved non-sensitive preferences may be processed by Google through the Gemini API to generate responses. AI-generated responses may not always be accurate. You may request assistance from a human representative. Our assistant is automated and does not claim to be a human representative.

4. Third-Party Services

Depending on the feature being used, information may be shared with:

  • Meta / Instagram: To receive and respond to Instagram messages and retrieve permitted account information.
  • Google (Gemini API): To process relevant conversation context and generate AI-assisted responses.
  • Razorpay: To generate payment QR codes and verify transactions.
  • Resend: To send relevant order and customer notifications to our designated business email.
  • Database and hosting providers: PostgreSQL-compatible database infrastructure and Vercel hosting to store and process application data.

These providers process information under their own privacy policies and applicable contractual obligations. We do not sell customer personal information.

5. Instagram Platform Data

Our application uses Meta's Instagram APIs and webhook services to receive and respond to messages from people who contact our Instagram account. We intend to process only information needed to provide messaging, customer support, and order-related services. We do not use Instagram information to sell personal data, build unrelated advertising profiles, or access private account information without authorization. Your use of Instagram is also governed by Meta's terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including restricted dashboard access, server-side storage of API credentials, webhook signature verification, payment verification with Razorpay, and secure connections where supported by our providers. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to manage conversations and orders, provide customer service, resolve disputes, meet applicable legal and accounting obligations, and prevent fraud. We may delete or anonymize information when it is no longer required, subject to lawful retention requirements.
